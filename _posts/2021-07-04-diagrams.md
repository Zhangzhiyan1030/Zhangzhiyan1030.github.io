Here, I first stepped onto the campus of CUC—a truly beautiful place. I adored my teachers and classmates, and looking back now, it feels like the whole city of Beijing sparkled that day. But I must mention that we lived in Building 43 at CUC, built in 1952 and used as a men's dormitory until 2000. There was no elevator, and we lived on the sixth floor. Every time I climbed those stairs, I felt like I was ready to drop dead.
